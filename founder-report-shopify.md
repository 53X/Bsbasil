The website is now connected to your Shopify store. The shop cannot go live until the items in “Still needed from you” are in.

## Done

These are already in the website:

- The homepage and the catalog read products from Shopify. One list, one price.
- Tapping a product opens its own page, with a link you can share.
- That page shows photos, a video when you upload one, the price, size, colour, stock, description, size guide, and your shipping and return text.

## Still needed from you

1. **Your CC Avenue Merchant ID and Access Code.** The account needs to be active, with `bsbasil.com` saved as your website.
2. **Your real products:** name, photos, price, sizes, colours, and how many you have. Videos can be added later.
3. **Four shop rules:** the return rule, the shipping rate, cash on delivery yes or no, and whether prices include GST.

## What you will be able to do

After this work:

- Add a new product in Shopify, and it appears on the website.
- Change a price in Shopify, and the website shows the new price.
- Remove or hide a product in Shopify, and it leaves the website.
- Set how many pieces you have. When a size sells out, the website says so.

You will not need a developer for those everyday changes.

## What a product page will show

When a buyer taps a product, they will see:

- Photos
- Name and price
- Size choices
- Colour choices
- Whether that size and colour is in stock
- A short description
- Shipping, return, and refund rules
- A size guide
- Add to bag, and Buy now

The page will have its own address, for example `bsbasil.com/products/sunshine-romper`, so you can share one product with a customer.

## How you will run the shop

You will use two tools. Each has one job.

**Shopify** is where you manage the shop.

- Add or hide a product
- Change the price
- Upload photos
- Set sizes and colours
- Set how many you have in stock
- Read your orders
- Write the shipping, return, and refund text once

**CC Avenue** is where the money is collected. You have already registered. When a buyer pays, CC Avenue takes the payment, and the order shows up in Shopify so the stock can go down.

**This website** is what the buyer sees. It keeps the current BS Basil design. It shows whatever you have saved in Shopify.

You will not update products inside GoDaddy after this. GoDaddy is where the design was built. Product changes happen in Shopify.

---

## How a buyer will buy

1. They browse the homepage or the catalog, which look like they do now.
2. They tap a product and land on that product’s page.
3. They pick a size and a colour.
4. They add it to the bag.
5. They pay with the methods turned on in your CC Avenue account, such as card, UPI, or net banking.
6. You see the order in Shopify, and the stock for that size goes down.
